# Buttercup squash curry
## Things you need
- half a buttercup squash
- a can of coconut milk
- a block of extra firm tofu
- onions
- garlic
- curry-able veggies
- spices
- some oil
- soy sauce
- mirin

## Preparation
### Squash the buttercup
1. Cover half in oil, pepper, salt
2. Bake at 425 for 40 minutes, until inside is soft
3. Remove, scoop out flesh (should already look like a mash)

### Prep the tofu
1. Cut tofu into slices
2. Sautee with some oil
3. Finish with a cap of soy saude

### Do the veggies
1. Sweat onion slices
2. Add crushed garlic
3. Add strips of veggies
4. Season

### Combine
1. Buttercup mash goes into a hot pan
2. Spices
3. Stir in coconut milk until saucy
4. Pour over cooked veggies and tofu, preserving half a cup
5. Deglazee veggies with sauce and some mirin

## Serve
Over rice
